nepos-tiles

Graph tiles for the routing in Nepos, built monthly from OpenStreetMap: the roads a bus may use in Norway with the car ferries, and the rail, tram, metro, ferry and cable networks.

Manifests: road, rail, tram, metro, ferry, cable. Source and format: github.com/johanwzh/nepos-tiles.
